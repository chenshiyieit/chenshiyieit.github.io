Welcome. I am **Shiyi Chen**, President and Chair Professor at the **Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo**, and a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research is concerned with **turbulence and computational fluid dynamics**: understanding the structures and statistical properties of turbulent flows, and developing computational methods that connect fundamental physics with engineering applications.

Our work spans lattice Boltzmann methods, direct numerical simulation, large-eddy simulation, multiphase flows and multiscale modelling. We are also interested in scientific computing and industrial software, bringing mathematical ideas and computational tools to complex fluid systems.

You can explore our [research](/en/research/), meet the [people](/en/people/), or browse the [publications](/en/publications/). Academic exchanges and collaborations are welcome.
