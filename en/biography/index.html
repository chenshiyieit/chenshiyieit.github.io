Shiyi Chen · 陈十一

Biography

Shiyi Chen is President and Chair Professor at the Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo. He is a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and The World Academy of Sciences, and a Fellow of the American Physical Society and the Institute of Physics.

His research focuses on turbulence theory and computational fluid dynamics. His work with collaborators includes the statistics and structure of turbulent velocity and passive scalar fields, mapping closure, the physical mechanism of the two-dimensional inverse energy cascade, lattice Boltzmann methods, multiscale hybrid algorithms and constrained large-eddy simulation.
